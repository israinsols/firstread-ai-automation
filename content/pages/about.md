Firstread.co.uk is an independent publication for readers interested in celebrity, news, technology, sport, lifestyle, law and food. We publish practical explainers alongside stories across these subjects, with a focus on clear information and useful sources.

## What readers can expect

Our sections bring together culture and entertainment, current affairs, technology, sport, everyday life, legal information and food. We value clear explanations and honest limitations over keyword-stuffed pages or claims that a tool, product or trick guarantees a result.

## Editorial approach

Information can change and may vary by location or situation. Readers should confirm important details with current, authoritative sources before acting. Our articles provide general information, not personalised professional advice.

If you spot a material error, contact us with the page link and a reliable reference so we can review it.
